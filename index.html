🎥 How to film

  • Camera at hip height, on a tripod or propped up, 3–4 m (10–12 ft) away.
  • Whole body in frame from address to finish: head, hands, club and feet. Leave space above the head for the club at the top.
  • Face-on: camera square to your chest, in line with the ball. Down-the-line: camera behind you, on your hand line, pointing at the target.
  • 60 fps or higher if your phone allows it; good light reduces blur.
  • Plain background, fitted clothing, and only one person in shot.
  • Keep the clip short: a second before takeaway to a second after the finish. (Live auto-capture trims for you.)
  • Live auto-capture on a phone needs the page served over https (or localhost). Camera access is blocked on plain http and on files opened from the phone's storage.
About accuracy: this tool estimates joint positions from a flat 2D video. Angles and distances are approximate (often ±5–10°) and depend on camera angle, lens and clothing. Use it to spot trends between your own swings, not as a precise measurement or a substitute for a coach or launch monitor.
